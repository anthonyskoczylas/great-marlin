The day

The coast goes gold. The heat lets go.

The sunset cruise is an easy evening run on the Great Marlin: the last hour of light and the coast from the water, then home in the dark.

Same cockpit as the fishing days, no rods, easy clothes. A good way to start a week, or to end one.

An easy evening run.

No early start, no lines in the water. Just the coast at the best hour of the day.

What’s included

Aboard

  • The whole boat and crew, your group only.
  • Shade, berths and a private head, below deck.

Bring

  • A layer, for the run home in the dark.
  • A camera, the light does the work.

Questions

  • How much is it?

    The sunset cruise is priced on request. Send your dates and group size and you get a straight number back.

  • How many people can come?

    Tell us your group when you ask. It is the whole boat, so nobody else joins.

Before you book

  • A 50% deposit holds your date.

    The balance is settled the day of the trip.

  • Cancel more than 14 days out

    and the deposit comes back to you.

  • Cancel inside 14 days

    and the deposit is kept. The date was held for you.

  • Rain or shine, the boat runs.

    Only weather that closes the port calls off a day.
