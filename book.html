Which day?

Every price is for your group, not per head. Every trip includes up to six guests. Fishing days: $100 each beyond six, up to eight aboard. Every other day: $40 each beyond six, up to ten.

When, and how many aboard?

Dates can stay open — we’ll tell you what the boat has. Kids count as aboard; they nap below deck when they’re done.

Door to the beach.

We meet on the beach in Herradura at 06:45 and a small panga runs you out to the boat. We can set up hotel and airport rides too, so the morning starts at your door if you want it to.

How payment works

  1. We confirm the date. Nothing is charged until we answer.
  2. 50% deposit holds it, or pay in full. Zelle, Venmo or PayPal. The details come in our reply.
  3. Paid the deposit? Settle the rest your way. Cash in US dollars on the beach, or Zelle, Venmo or PayPal before the day.

Cancel 10 or more days out and everything you paid comes back to you; inside 10 days the 50% deposit is kept and anything above it comes back. If the captain or the port calls the day off for safety, we move you to another date or refund everything you paid.
