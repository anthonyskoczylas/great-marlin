Which day?

Every price is for your group, not per head. Fishing days seat six, each guest beyond is $100. Surf and island days run to ten, each guest past the base $40.

When, and how many aboard?

Dates can stay open — we’ll tell you what the boat has. Kids count as aboard; they nap below deck when they’re done.
