The day

Your boat, your group, the island.

Tortuga Island sits out in the Gulf of Nicoya, across the water from Herradura. We take you there on the Great Marlin: white sand, water clear enough to snorkel, and a day that needs no rods.

This one is not brokered. It runs on our own boat, with your group and nobody else.

A day that needs no rods.

Same boat, same crew, easy clothes.

What’s included

Aboard

  • The boat and crew, your group only.
  • Up to eight guests, at the base price; $40 for each guest beyond, up to ten.
  • Shade, berths and a private head, below deck.

Bring

  • Swimwear and a towel, the water is the point.
  • Sun cover, hat, sunglasses, sunscreen.
  • Seasickness medication, if boats get to you.

Questions

  • How many people can come?

    The price covers up to eight guests. Each guest beyond eight is $40, up to ten.

  • Is there fishing on the way?

    This is the no-rods day. If you want fishing too, ask when you book and we will tell you honestly what fits.

  • What should we ask before booking?

    Anything at all: timing, what to bring, the group. One message gets a straight answer.

Before you book

  • A 50% deposit holds your date.

    The balance is settled the day of the trip.

  • Cancel more than 14 days out

    and the deposit comes back to you.

  • Cancel inside 14 days

    and the deposit is kept. The date was held for you.

  • Rain or shine, the boat runs.

    Only weather that closes the port calls off a day.
