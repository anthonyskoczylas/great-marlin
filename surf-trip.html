Surf day by boat

A reef we don’t name. White sand, an A-frame over reef, and no road in. Boat access only.

The day

Empty on purpose.

The surf day runs on the Great Marlin out of Herradura to a white-sand beach with an A-frame reef wave that you can only reach by boat. No road in, which is the whole point.

We don’t print its name. “A reef we don’t name” is the trip. You will see it when you get there.

Six hours on the water, provisioned like the full day: lunch, beer, water and fruit aboard.

No road in.

The only way there is the boat, and the boat is yours for the day.

What’s included

Aboard

  • The boat and crew, your group only.
  • Six hours, out of Herradura and back.
  • Lunch, beer, water and fruit, aboard.
  • Shade, berths and a private head, below deck between sessions.

Bring

  • Your board, and whatever you surf in.
  • Sun cover, hat, sunglasses, sunscreen.
  • Seasickness medication, if boats get to you.

Questions

  • Where is the wave?

    Close to Los Sueños, reachable only by boat. We keep the name off the internet so it stays the way it is.

  • How many people can come?

    The price covers up to six guests. Each guest beyond that is $40, up to ten.

  • Can we combine surfing and fishing?

    Tell us when you book and we will tell you honestly what fits in the day.

Before you book

  • A 50% deposit holds your date.

    The balance is settled the day of the trip.

  • Cancel more than 14 days out

    and the deposit comes back to you.

  • Cancel inside 14 days

    and the deposit is kept. The date was held for you.

  • Rain or shine, the boat runs.

    Only weather that closes the port calls off a day.
